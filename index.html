    Orange-red shading marks slopes of 30° or more (swisstopo, 10 m model). Avalanches release on slopes from about 30°, so keep the tent away from them and from the runout below them.

    Colours come from the source layers (federal hunting reserves, wildlife quiet zones, floodplains, bogs and more). A coloured area is not always a ban: tap the spot to see what applies.

    Cantonal reserve data (BE, TI, VS, GE, GL, FR, LU, SO, JU) is checked when you tap a spot but is not drawn on the map.

    Offline: the saved map and bundled data still work. Zone, canton, water and weather lookups need a connection, so results are incomplete.
