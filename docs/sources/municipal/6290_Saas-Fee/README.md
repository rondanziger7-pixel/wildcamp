# Saas-Fee (BFS 6290): camping rules

- Polizeireglement der Gemeinde Saas-Fee, adopted by the Urversammlung on 15 December 2025 and homologated by the Staatsrat of Valais on 22 April 2026: https://www.3906.ch/_rte/publikation/13186 (retrieved 2026-10-04). The saved text is OCR of the scanned PDF (`Polizeireglement_2025-12-15.txt`), so words are sometimes garbled ("Zeiten" for "Zelten").
  - Art. 29 Campieren: para. 1 is worded as an offence: whoever "auf öffentlichem Grund und Boden ausserhalb der von der Gemeinde dafür bezeichneten Zonen campiert". Para. 2: "Campieren" is temporary stay in tents, caravans or similar mobile and immobile installations; merely pitching tents or caravans also counts.
  - Art. 47: fine CHF 10 to 5,000, doubled up to 10,000 if an unlawful state is not remedied; higher penalties under cantonal or federal law stay reserved.
- Not addressed: sleeping outdoors without a tent; private land (the article covers public ground only).
- The OCR drops the lead-in of Art. 29 para. 1, so the offence wording is inferred from the article's structure, which is why the rule is recorded as `restricted` (caution), not `banned`.
