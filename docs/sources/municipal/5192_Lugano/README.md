# Lugano (BFS 5192): camping rules

BFS 5192 verified 2026-10-04 against swissBOUNDARIES3D (geo.admin identify, point E 2717950 / N 1096100 -> gde_nr 5192, gemname Lugano; the second hit, 5172 Castagnola, is an older merged municipality).

Official legislation list: https://www.lugano.ch/la-mia-citta/amministrazione/legislazione.html (retrieved 2026-10-04; 122 documents under /law/... downloaded with curl and converted with pdftotext -layout, text layer fine, no OCR; every document was keyword searched: campegg-, pernott-, accampar-, bivacc-, dormire, roulotte, caravan, camper). Lugano has NO general Regolamento/Ordinanza di polizia in its collection (only ROPol, the police corps staff regulation, and fee regulations).

- 2.5.1 Ordinanza municipale sui parchi urbani, giardini pubblici e campi da gioco del 5 novembre 2015 (Art. 1 cpv. 2 added by Municipio resolution 7.11.2024, displayed 12.11.-12.12.2024): https://www.lugano.ch/law/16176729/2-5-1-Ordinanza-municipale-sui-parchi-urbani-giardini-pubblici-e-campi-da-gioco-del-5-novembre-2015 . Saved `Ordinanza-parchi-urbani-giardini-pubblici_2024-11-07.txt`.
  - Art. 13 cpv. 1: "È vietato campeggiare o pernottare nei parchi, nei giardini pubblici e nei campi da gioco."
  - Art. 2 cpv. 1 defines parks/public gardens as "aree verdi preservate dall'urbanizzazione, provviste di prati, piante ornamentali o alberature".
  - Art. 19 cpv. 1: "Le infrazioni alla presente Ordinanza sono punite con la multa fino a CHF 10'000.--." Procedure LOC art. 145 ss. (in force at the end of the display period; the ordinance's Art. 20 refers to art. 208 LOC).
  - Art. 12: bathing banned within 100 m of harbour entrances in lakeside parks (not camping).
- 2.5.15 Ordinanza municipale sul Parco San Grato del 7 novembre 2024 (displayed 11.11.-11.12.2024): https://www.lugano.ch/law/31199788/2-5-15-Ordinanza-municipale-sul-Parco-San-Grato-del-7-novembre-2024 . Art. 10 cpv. 1: "È vietato campeggiare o pernottare nel parco." Art. 15 cpv. 1: fine up to CHF 10'000. Saved `Ordinanza-Parco-San-Grato_2024-11-07.txt`.
- 2.2.5 Ordinanza municipale sull'uso dell'area per camper dell'8 ottobre 2020 (area at part. 1236 RFD, Tassino, established "per la durata di cinque anni" - so the five years ran out in about late 2025; the ordinance is still listed): Art. 3 cpv. 2 overnight stay allowed in campers only; Art. 4 lett. d bans "qualsiasi atteggiamento che crei o simuli una situazione di campeggio"; Art. 8 fine up to CHF 10'000. Saved `Ordinanza-area-camper_2020-10-08.txt`.
- 2.1.1 Regolamento sui beni amministrativi del 30.1.1989 (last amended, approved 16.2.2026), https://www.lugano.ch/law/16174911/... : Art. 5 free common use "conformemente alla loro destinazione"; Art. 7 cpv. 2 permit needed for "la posa di tende, ombrelloni, ... la sosta di veicoli" (use of little intensity). No camping article. Saved `Regolamento-beni-amministrativi_2026-02-16.txt`.
- Fee ordinances list camping sites ("Campeggi, per posto tenda o camper") only in the waste-fee regulation (4.1): not a camping rule.

Not addressed / notes:
- Streets, squares, lake shores outside parks, forests/public land outside parks, private land and sleeping outdoors without a tent / in a vehicle outside the camper area are NOT covered by any Lugano municipal text found. Only parks/gardens/play fields and Parco San Grato are banned. (Cantonal Legge sui campeggi is already recorded in the app.)
- The ban says "campeggiare o pernottare", so it also covers overnighting in the park without a tent.
- "Parchi urbani" is a defined category; whether a given lakeside lawn counts as a park (e.g. Parco Ciani) is a factual question. Version date of 2.5.1: the base text is 5.11.2015, latest amendment 7.11.2024 (Art. 13 unchanged).
