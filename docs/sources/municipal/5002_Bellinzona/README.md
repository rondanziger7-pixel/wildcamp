# Bellinzona (BFS 5002): camping rules

BFS 5002 verified 2026-10-04 against swissBOUNDARIES3D (geo.admin identify, point E 2722350 / N 1117800 -> gde_nr 5002, gemname Bellinzona).

Official legislation index "Legislazione comunale": https://www.bellinzona.ch/1d212c00 (retrieved 2026-10-04; the site returned a transient 503 error page once, later requests fine). All 87 documents listed there were downloaded with curl (each URL returns a PDF) and converted with pdftotext -layout; the index with titles is `_index-of-87-documents-checked.tsv`. Six PDFs had no text layer (Regolamento Istituto scolastico, Via Bonasco residential zone, three parking-contribution ordinances of Claro/Gnosca/Preonzo, water installations ordinance); OCR was not run because their titles are unrelated to camping.

Result: NO municipal camping/tenting/overnighting rule found. Bellinzona (merged 2017) has no Regolamento/Ordinanza di polizia in its collection; police is mentioned only in the Regolamento comunale (the Municipio "esercita le funzioni di polizia locale", fees "stabilite da regolamenti speciali") and in fee ordinances. Keyword searches (campegg-, pernott-, accampar-, bivacc-, dormire, roulotte, caravan, camper) over all texts hit only:
- Regolamento comunale (adopted by Consiglio comunale 16/17.4.2018, approved by the Cantone 28.6.2018): https://www.bellinzona.ch/Regolamento-comunale-Citt-di-Bellinzona-4e93e800 . Saved `Regolamento-comunale_2018-06-28.txt`. Art. 61 (Attribuzioni del Municipio) lett. f: "esercita le funzioni di polizia locale". No camping article.
- Ordinanza municipale concernente l'uso dell'area camper ubicata al mapp. no. 187 RFD, Centro Sportivo (adopted 13.4.2011, in force 17.5.2011), `Ordinanza-area-camper-Centro-Sportivo_2011-05-17.txt`: Art. 3 lett. a "L'area può essere utilizzata unicamente per il parcheggio di camper, nel quale è permesso il pernottamento"; Art. 5 lett. d bans "l'assunzione di qualsiasi atteggiamento che crei o simuli una situazione di campeggio"; Art. 9: contravvenzione procedure LOC art. 145 ss. Only for that area.
- Waste regulation (Regolamento comunale sulla gestione dei rifiuti and its OM): "Campeggi" only as a fee category (CHF 1'000-2'000 / CHF 1'500).
- Zone-protection regulation Gorduno-Gnosca (water protection): table row "Campeggi per tende, aree per roulotte e Camper" marked not allowed in a zone (+ / - / -), i.e. a water-protection zone rule for the wells, not a town-wide camping rule (not saved; see the 75th entry of the index).
- Also read, no camping content: Regolamento occupazione area pubblica (in force 1.1.2015, only awnings "tende"), OM salvaguardia area di uso pubblico (littering, 13.4.2015), OM bagno pubblico (4.5.1970, republished 1995), OM rumori molesti (1.7.2007).

Not addressed: everything else (public ground, parks, lake/river shores, sleeping outdoors without a tent, vehicles, private land). Absence of a municipal rule does not mean camping is legal: cantonal law (Legge sui campeggi, already recorded) and federal/cantonal forest and nature-protection law still apply. Doubts: the index might not include rules of former municipalities that are not listed (only Camorino/Claro/Giubiasco regulations on other topics were listed); the six non-OCR'd PDFs were not read.
