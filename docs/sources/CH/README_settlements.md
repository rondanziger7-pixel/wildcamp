# Federal law on camping in and near settlements (read 2026-10-04)

No federal statute bans camping in built-up areas, and none allows it. What the federal texts do settle:

- **Art. 699 para. 1 ZGB**: "Das Betreten von Wald und Weide und die Aneignung wildwachsender Beeren, Pilze u. dgl. sind in ortsüblichem Umfange jedermann gestattet, soweit nicht im Interesse der Kulturen seitens der zuständigen Behörde einzelne bestimmt umgrenzte Verbote erlassen werden." The public access right covers **forest and pasture** only, and only to the extent usual locally. Gardens, fields, yards and built-up land are not covered, so staying there needs the owner's consent.
- **Art. 641 para. 2 ZGB**: the owner may "jede ungerechtfertigte Einwirkung abwehren".
- **Art. 186 StGB** (Hausfriedensbruch, only on complaint): entering or staying against the will of the entitled person in a house, a closed room, or "einen unmittelbar zu einem Hause gehörenden umfriedeten Platz, Hof oder Garten" is punishable with up to three years or a fine. An unfenced meadow is not covered by it, which is why the app speaks of consent and municipal rules, not of a crime.
- **Art. 15 para. 1 RPG** defines building zones (Bauzonen) as land set aside for development; the national harmonised map of them is `ch.are.bauzonen` (ARE). Municipal police regulations, which are where camping bans on public ground sit, are checked per municipality (see `docs/sources/municipal/`).
