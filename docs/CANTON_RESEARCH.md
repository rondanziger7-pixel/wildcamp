# Canton wild-camping rules: research log

Last updated 2026-10-04. The source texts are saved in `docs/sources/<canton code>/` so every rule in `src/cantons.ts` can be checked against what was read. Pages were fetched over plain HTTPS from the sandbox (`scripts/fetch_lexwork.py` for cantonal law portals built on LexWork; `curl` + `pdftotext` for PDFs). The research assistant's page-fetch tool was blocked for every site, so web-search summaries were used only to *find* documents, never as the source of a rule.

## Rules in the app (read from the primary text)
| Canton | Stance | Source read | Gist |
|---|---|---|---|
| OW | restricted | Gesetz über das Campieren (GDB 971.4), in force 2015-03-01 | Tents/caravans/motorhomes outside approved campsites banned (Art. 6); a single night without permit allowed if no public or private interests are impaired (Art. 8); fines (Art. 11) |
| NW | restricted | Cantonal leaflet, 2021-11-18, §3.11 (guidance, not statute); Biotope ordinance NG 332.11 | In principle not allowed without landowner consent; single nights above the forest line (not groups) as exception; general ban in nature reserves, federal hunting reserves, wildlife quiet zones; tents banned off-path in protected bogs and dry sites |
| TI | restricted (tolerated above the treeline) | Legge sui campeggi, 2004-01-26 (state 2024-01-01) | Camping only in authorised campsites (Art. 2 para. 1); Art. 2 para. 2 excepts "l'attendamento a scopo di bivacco in montagna"; "mountains" undefined, so the app reads above the treeline as mountains; fines CHF 50 to 10,000 (Art. 27) |
| VD | restricted | Loi sur les campings et caravanings résidentiels (LCCR, RSV 935.61), Art. 27 and 44 | Occasional camping outside authorised places only with the landowner's (or farmer's/tenant's) consent; over 4 days needs the commune's authorisation; communal rules reserved; fines up to CHF 10,000. **Caveat:** the text is the official BLV export dated 2021-10-05 obtained through a mirror, because the official portal could not be scripted; amendments since then were not checked |
| AI | tolerated | Standeskommission statement, 2025-06-18 | The canton says there is currently *no legal basis* regulating bivouacking and plans to create a general ban (landowner permission, alpine-farm agreement). A policy direction, not yet law |
| SG | tolerated | Kantonsforstamt leaflet, 2022-05-02, §3.6 (guidance) | Individuals and small groups mostly allowed; not in federal hunting reserves, nature reserves, municipal protection areas; avoid the upper forest line, floodplains, wetlands |

`restricted` caps the verdict at "Caution"; `tolerated` adds the note only. Leaflets are cantonal *guidance*, which the summaries say.

## Read, but no general camping rule found
Absence from the texts below is not proof that no rule exists elsewhere (municipal police regulations, reserve decrees, other laws).
- **GR**: Police Act (BR 613.000) and Police Ordinance (BR 613.100) contain no camping provisions. Consistent with reports that municipalities decide. Municipal rules not checked.
- **UR**: Campingverordnung (RB 70.2431) covers commercial campsites only. A separate reserve ordinance (RB 10.5110) bans camping on the Urnersee south shore.
- **BE**: Kantonale Waldverordnung (BSG 921.111) and Naturschutzverordnung (BSG 426.111) contain no camping provisions. Individual reserve decrees (e.g. Napf) reportedly ban camping, and the *city* of Bern bans it on public land (media, not read).
- **VS**: Construction ordinance (OC 705.100) requires a building permit for tents or caravans outside a campsite only if they stay over 3 weeks or number more than 12, so it does not govern a hiker's tent. The cantonal master plan sheet B.3 (a **draft** modification with tracked changes) states as planning policy that camping outside suitable zones should be prohibited except short special cases and youth camps with landowner and commune consent, and that communes set wild-camping rules in their police regulations. That is not a statute binding hikers, so no rule is recorded.
- **FR**: Forest law (RSF 921.1/921.11) and nature law (RSF 721.0.1) contain no camping provisions. Official web pages recommend camping "only where expressly authorised" in forests and biotopes and ban "staying" (with or without a tent) in wildlife quiet zones. These are recommendations and a restatement of the quiet-zone layer, so no rule is recorded.
- **JU**: official statements (2023 communiqué, Doubs reserve page) say overnighting outside official campsites is prohibited in all nature reserves, and the fines ordinance (OLiLAO 324.111, item 1.6) sets a CHF 100 fine for camping in the Doubs reserve. No general cantonal ban or permission was found. The media claim that Jura is "generally allowed" is unverified.

## Also read, no general rule for a hiker's tent
- **NE**: Arrêté 727.3 (1963) requires department authorisation to install a camping tent or caravan, but only on land owned by the State (fine up to CHF 500 on State public domain). Ownership of the land at a spot is unknown, so no rule is recorded. A reported ban in the Creux du Van reserve was not read.
- **LU**: PBG § 174 covers regular camping and campsites (building permit above 30 days) and says nothing about a single overnight stay.
- **SZ**: Law 543.110 (1959) covers public campsite permits only. Camping in federal hunting reserves is banned federally (VEJ).
- **ZH**: the Regierungsrat decree on tenting and camping (LS 702.112, 1964) was repealed on 2003-10-01. Zürich's nature-protection areas ban camping (reported, not read).

## Not found or not researched
- A web-search summary that attributed Vaud's camping law to Valais was wrong and was not used.
- **GE, BS, AG, AR, BL, GL, SH, SO, TG, ZG**: no cantonal camping rule found or not read. Geneva is reported to ban camping in forest except designated places with authorisation (not read). Reports that Aargau allows one night are unverified (one article calls the claim questionable). Glarus reportedly relies on protected-area bans and dialogue (media).

## Cantonal nature reserves
- **Bern**: 244 reserve perimeters from the canton's geoservice (`a42geo/of_environment01_de_ms_wms`, layer NSG_NSGP). Every record links its protection decree on the ÖREB file store. All 244 decrees were downloaded; the 15 scans without a text layer were OCR'd (RapidOCR; text saved in `docs/sources/BE/ocr/`). Result (`docs/sources/BE/reserves_decree_scan.csv`): **181** name camping or tenting as prohibited (German or French wording, including heavily garbled OCR), **20** prohibit entering the reserve (or leaving the marked paths) without naming camping, **43** are old decrees with no camping clause, **0** unreadable. How it was checked: every reserve that was not an obvious German "das Campieren / das Aufstellen von Zelten" match had its context read (the 26 reserves newly resolved by French terms, hyphenation fixes and OCR were all read, and all 20 entry-ban matches), plus a random sample of 20 ordinary matches. A first version of the classifier required an exact "untersagt" and flipped 12 decrees whose OCR had mangled that word; the final one tolerates OCR damage and no previously banned reserve lost its status. Two reserves allow camping only at designated places: Sense-Schwarzwasser (no. 55, places set by the municipalities) and Suldtal (no. 58, specially designated places agreed with the landowners). Rebuild with `scripts/build_be_reserves.py`.
- **Ticino**: WFS layer `ti_002_1_v1_0_decreti_protezione` at `wfs.geo.ti.ch` (351 polygons in force: 260 "Riserva naturale", 91 "Zona di protezione della natura"). Each polygon links its decree; there are only 26 distinct decrees. All 26 were downloaded (text layers present, up to 170 pages) and searched in Italian. A camping term counts only with a prohibition wording in the preceding 1,800 characters or as a lettered list item, never in the table of contents. **15 decrees prohibit camping**: Alpe Vicania, the Tresa amphibian sites, Foce della Magliasina, Legiuna (Art. 27 "Il campeggio è vietato all'interno dell'intera Zona protetta"), Valle Bavona (Art. 19, same wording), Brenno, Tresa, Bedretto, Maggia, Molino–Colombera, Pre Murin–Vigna–Pavü, prato secco Ex Aerodromo, Bolle di Magadino (ordinance, art. g, except zone C sectors with a cantonal authorisation), Origlio, and the national bogs plan. Brenno, Bedretto and Maggia ban camping outside areas authorised for occasional camping under Art. 5 of the camping law. **11 decrees have no camping prohibition** (the Magliasina floodplain only mentions camping in its explanatory report). The first classifier versions disagreed with my own reading of the articles twice (an over-strict version and one that lost long lists); the final one matches my reading of all 26. The 808 *provisional* perimeters in the separate area layer (mostly nationally important bogs, already covered by the federal inventories) are not included. Rebuild with `scripts/build_ti_reserves.py`; excerpts are in `docs/sources/TI/reserves_decree_scan.csv`.
- **Graubünden**: `wfs.geo.gr.ch/naturschutz` has biotopes, geotopes, parks, landscapes and the federal inventories, but no separate cantonal nature-reserve layer, so nothing was added.
- **Valais**: ArcGIS layer `sit.vs.ch/arcgis/rest/services/Nature/MapServer/1101` ("Sites protégés par décisions cantonales"): 100 polygons, each with the number of its decision on `lex.vs.ch` (49 distinct decisions). The texts were fetched from the portal's JSON API and searched in French and German. **12 decisions prohibit camping or tenting** (Aletschwald, Derborence, Pfynwald, Achera Biela, Lac de Morgins, Marais d'Ardon et Chamoson, Bilderne, Chiemadmatte, Lotrey, Barme, Ar du Tsan, Feegletscher); **3 prohibit entering the marsh** or leaving the marked paths (La Maraîche de Plex, Flesch, Marais de Champex); **34 list prohibited activities without naming camping** (they stay "Caution"). A first version wrongly counted Sand (451.326), whose decision says the commune "peut ... autoriser temporairement le camping": a permission, not a ban. Final results were checked by reading all 12 camping matches, the 3 entry matches and the first prohibited-activity wording of every silent decision. This is separate from the (still unrecorded) cantonal statute for hikers' tents: Valais has none that I could find. Rebuild with `scripts/build_vs_reserves.py`.
- **Geneva** (`reserves-ge.json.gz`, 68 sites): polygons from the canton's open-data service; the regulation on nature reserves (RPPMF, `silgeneve.ch/legis/data/rsg_l4_05p11.htm`, Art. 19) prohibits all activities in nature reserves except management and limits visitors to signed paths, so camping is prohibited there. Sites that are not nature reserves in that sense stay "Caution". Rebuild: `scripts/build_cantonal_reserves.py GE`.
- **Glarus** (`reserves-gl.json.gz`): polygons of the cantonal protection areas (`ch.gl.natureprotection.nsg-perimeter`); their rules were not read, so "Caution" only.
- **Fribourg** (`reserves-fr.json.gz`, 18 reserves): polygons only; the rules were not read, so "Caution" only. Page with the legal context: `fr.ch/diaf/sfn/...`.
- **Lucerne** (`reserves-lu.json.gz`, 20 areas): polygons from the canton's service, each linked to its protection ordinance on `srl.lu.ch` (LexWork). 18 ordinances prohibit camping or tenting or entering; 2 are silent. Excerpts in `docs/sources/LU/reserves_ordinance_scan.csv`. Rebuild: `scripts/build_lu_reserves.py`.
- **Solothurn** (`reserves-so.json.gz`, 101 reserves): WFS `ch.so.arp.naturreservate.reservate` at `geo.so.ch/api/wfs`, each reserve listing its legal documents; the 143 distinct in-force PDFs were downloaded, scans OCR'd and classified like the Bern decrees. **11 reserves prohibit camping ("das Campieren" in the list of prohibited acts)**, 87 do not name it, 3 have no readable document. Excerpts in `docs/sources/SO/reserves_document_scan.csv`. One match was rejected after reading its context: 1.02 Aareufer Mutten only mentions an existing campsite. Several OCR texts are garbled (2.11, 2.17); the camping match sits in a list with other prohibited acts, but those two were not confirmed against the scans by eye. The server ignores the requested projection and returns lon/lat, so the builder converts to LV95 itself (a first build stored unconverted coordinates; tests now use points taken from the source). Rebuild: `scripts/build_so_reserves.py`.
- **Nothing usable found**: AG, AI, AR, BL, BS, GR (federal inventories and biotopes only), NE, NW, OW, SG (the WFS id I found was the land-use plan), SH, SZ, TG, UR, VD, ZG, ZH. Reasons varied: viewer-only maps (WMS without feature data), blocked scripted access, no cantonal reserve layer, or I could not locate a service. This is not evidence that these cantons have no reserves or no bans.
- **Jura**: the canton's WMS layer `env_04_01` mixes nature reserves and landscape protection zones; only `tri` = "Réserve naturelle…" counts. The official statement (2023 communiqué) says overnighting outside official campsites is prohibited in all nature reserves. The reserves are queried live (`src/jura.ts`) and the point is tested against the polygon the service returns. The service was slow and reset connections after a burst of my own test requests, so a failure degrades to "no Jura reserve data" rather than breaking the result.

## Municipal rules
- **City of Bern (BFS 351)**: Campingverordnung SSSB 732.221 (state 1.11.2012, still current on `stadtrecht.bern.ch`). Art. 2 bans pitching tents, caravans or similar and camping on the city's public ground outside designated areas; Art. 3 exceptions; Art. 5 fines up to CHF 2,000. Art. 1 limits it to the city's public ground, so it only caps at "Caution". Text saved in `docs/sources/municipal/351_Bern/`.
- **Bernese Oberland (17 municipalities read from their own regulation pages, 2026-10-04; BFS numbers checked against swissBOUNDARIES3D):**

## Federal texts read (saved in docs/sources/CH/)
- **VEJ (SR 922.31), Art. 5 para. 1 let. e**: "Das freie Zelten und Campieren ist verboten. Vorbehalten bleibt die Benutzung offizieller Zeltplätze. Die Kantone können Ausnahmen bewilligen." Confirms federal hunting reserves are a legal ban (cantons can grant exceptions).
- **WZVV (SR 922.32)**: has **no** general camping or tenting clause. Reserve-specific provisions sit in the federal inventory's object sheets. A web-search summary claimed otherwise; the ordinance text does not support it. Federal bird reserves therefore only trigger "Caution".

## Federal and cross-cutting points seen in official texts
- Free entry to forest and pasture in customary measure: ZGB Art. 699. Uses disadvantageous to the forest are not allowed: WaG Art. 16 (cited in the SG leaflet).
- SG's guidance says to avoid the upper forest line as sensitive habitat, so "above the treeline is fine" is not universal advice.

## Adding or updating a rule
1. Save the source: `python3 scripts/fetch_lexwork.py <host> <number> docs/sources/<CODE>/<number>.md [lang]` for LexWork portals, or download the PDF and `pdftotext -layout`.
2. Read it. Quote article numbers in the summary and say if it is guidance rather than law.
3. Add the rule to `src/cantons.ts`. `npm test` rejects sources that are not on the canton's official domain (or admin.ch), bad dates, and rules with no saved source text.
4. Municipal rules are out of scope; the app tells users to check with the municipality.

## Federal inventories: what the ordinances actually say (read 2026-10-04, saved in `docs/sources/CH/`)
- **AuenV (floodplains, glacier forefields)**, Hochmoorverordnung, Flachmoorverordnung, Moorlandschaftsverordnung, TwwV (dry meadows), AlgV (amphibian sites): none has a camping or tenting ban. AuenV Art. 5 para. 2 let. c, HMV Art. 5 para. 1 let. i and k, FMV Art. 5 para. 2 let. l and m, and MLV Art. 5 para. 2 let. e oblige the cantons to keep recreation compatible with (or subordinate to) the protection aim and to prevent trampling; TwwV Art. 6 and AlgV Art. 6 only require the objects to be preserved undiminished. The app previously treated floodplains, bogs, fens, dry meadows and amphibian sites as "Not allowed"; that overstated the federal law, so they are now "Caution".
- **Wildlife quiet zones**: the BAFU layer description says that in statutory zones only the permitted marked paths may be accessed, that recommended zones are suggestions, and that the dataset is incomplete because the cantons' progress differs. Each zone carries its status (`schutzs_de`), rule (`best_de`) and season (`schutzzeit`); the app uses them (see `src/geoadmin.ts`).
- Layers checked and added: `ch.bafu.bundesinventare-auen_anhang2` (provisional floodplain list), `ch.vbs.schiessanzeigen` (army shooting notices with dates), `ch.swisstopo.geologie-gletscherausdehnung` (glacier extent). Considered and not added: guard-dog pastures (`ch.bafu.alpweiden-herdenschutzhunde`), because the meaning of the layer's attributes was not clear to me.

## Swiss National Park
- The Nationalparkgesetz (SR 454, state 2017-01-01, text read) has no camping clause; Art. 7 hands the rules to the Parkordnung. The park's own page (nationalpark.ch/schutzbestimmungen, saved as `docs/sources/CH/454_Nationalpark_Schutzbestimmungen.txt`) states: stay is allowed only by day (civil twilight to civil twilight), the marked trails and rest places must not be left, no fires, and the park is closed in winter. So overnight camping is prohibited. I did not find the Parkordnung text itself on fedlex (the 1963 ordinance's PDF is not served).
- It is the only national park in Switzerland. The other parks of national importance (regional nature parks) were treated as having no blanket camping ban; I did not read each park's rules.
- Parco Nazionale della Val Grande is in Italy (Piedmont). A first version treated every point inside a rough Swiss bounding box as Swiss, so spots in Italy, France, Germany and Austria near the border could read "Likely OK". The app now says "Outside Switzerland" when the canton lookup finds no canton.

## Why bivouacking is banned at Oeschinensee, and where else it applies (read 2026-10-04)
Nothing federal or cantonal applies there: the lake lies in the BLN inventory and the UNESCO Jungfrau-Aletsch site (neither carries a camping fine) and in no federal hunting reserve, and the canton of Bern has no general camping ban. Two instruments make it illegal:
1. **Municipal police regulation of Kandersteg** (Gemeindepolizeireglement, in force 1.1.2021; text read, saved in `docs/sources/municipal/565_Kandersteg/`). Art. 7: camping outside designated, authorised areas is not permitted anywhere in the municipality (Art. 6 let. a counts forest, pasture and public waters as public space). Art. 26: fine up to CHF 5,000; the ordinance sets CHF 200. **Art. 6 let. c defines camping as overnight stays in tents, caravans, motorhomes, cars or similar and expressly excludes sleeping outdoors without a tent ("Biwakieren")**, so on its own the municipal rule does not cover a tentless bivouac.
2. **A court prohibition (richterliches Verbot, Art. 258 ZPO) obtained by the landowners** around the lake (Alpgenossenschaft Oeschinenholz and the railway, per local press). The lakeside operator's outdoor-guidelines page states that camping, bivouacking and overnight stays in hammocks are prohibited by court order; breaches can be fined up to CHF 2,000 (the ZPO maximum). I could not find the order itself or its perimeter online (both are on signs at the site), so this rests on the operator's published statement and the press. The app draws it as the lake plus 400 m ("Not allowed") and 400 m to 1.5 km ("Be careful, may be inside the posted area"): `scripts/build_court_bans.py`, `public/bans-court.json.gz`.

**Other places.** Municipal camping bans and landowners' court prohibitions are the two instruments that matter beyond nature-protection law, and they exist elsewhere: Bern city (already recorded); a court prohibition for the whole Stockhorn area (Stockhornbahn AG page: camping and overnighting in the terrain, fine up to CHF 2,000 under Art. 258 ZPO; no perimeter given); and a Glarus Kantonsgericht prohibition (case ZG.2024.00752, 16.12.2024, published in the Glarus Amtsblatt 8.1.2025) covering camping, bivouacking and drones on parcels 772 and 779 Linthal (Muttenwändli, Muttenalp, Muttenkopf, Muttenwand, near the Muttsee), which I know from one secondary article only. Stockhorn and Muttsee are not drawn in the app because their perimeters are not available to me, and I did not want to invent a shape. Municipal rules found for other municipalities are listed under "Municipal rules".
